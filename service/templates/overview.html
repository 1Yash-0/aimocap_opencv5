The problem

Studios are out of reach

Optical motion capture needs a dedicated lab, markers on the body and an operator. The equipment alone costs more than USD 150,000 (Uhlrich et al., 2023).

One camera is not enough

Single-camera AI apps estimate depth from one view, so the motion drifts and shakes.

So most people go without

Indie game developers, animators, physiotherapists and coaches are left without a practical way to capture motion.

How it works

  1. Find the cameras. OpenCV 5 finds a checkerboard in each phone's video and works out where each phone stands and points.
  2. Find the person. Three neural networks run by the OpenCV 5 DNN engine find 133 points on the body, hands and face in every frame of every phone.
  3. Line up the clocks. The phones start at different times. AIMoCap finds each phone's delay from the movement itself.
  4. Build the 3D skeleton. The views are fused into 3D, and a smoother removes shake without slowing fast movements.
  5. Export. The motion is saved as BVH, a standard animation file for Blender, Unreal Engine, Unity and similar tools.

Close to OpenCap on position error, with lower low-motion jitter and added hand and face tracking.
